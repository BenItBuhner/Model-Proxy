NIM re-measurement campaign — infrastructure findings (2026-09-16)

Why everything is re-measured: the inference proxy's previous backend (Nahcrof) proxied traffic to cheaper
models than requested, so every member baseline, candidate table and fusion number dated before 2026-09-16
was measured against downgraded upstreams. The backend is NVIDIA NIM from 2026-09-16 (census:
nim-endpoint-census.txt). Per-stream throughput on NIM: glm-5.3 6-9 tok/s, kimi-k3 14-21 (30-90 s queue
before the first token under load), deepseek-v4-flash 14-30, nemotron-3-ultra 35-118; a high-effort
reasoning trace of 30-60k tokens therefore takes 30-90 minutes on the reasoning members.

Defects the slow upstream exposed in the measurement stack, each fixed before any number below was kept:

1. Bun's fetch aborts a request after 300 s without socket activity ("The operation timed out."); the
   proxy's upstream fetch did not disable it. A NIM-queued stream with no bytes yet died at exactly 300 s,
   the error classified as a key failure, and the single key's cooldown then failed every other worker of
   the wave instantly. Fixed: `timeout: false` on every upstream fetch, TimeoutError classified as
   fallback-without-cooldown (tests in provider-stream-hygiene.test.ts).
2. Route cooldowns on a single-route provider are blackholes: one upstream 524/5xx/socket close put the
   key into a 10 s cooldown and 44 queued BBEH items failed in 5 ms each. Fixed: provider error handling
   500/502/503/504/524 → fallback_no_cooldown, route cooldown 1 s (bootstrap-fusion-max.ts).
3. The upstream proxy has its own cooldown answer ("All 0 routes failed for model X") under load. Kernel:
   a proposer that fails within a minute without streaming is re-dispatched after 30/60 s
   (worker_fast_failure_retries); kernel-bench and the SWE harness retry transient failures in-run.
4. Cloudflare returns 524 for a non-streaming origin response slower than 100 s; the SWE harness's agent
   steps were non-streaming and every slow model's step died. Fixed: steps stream and assemble tool calls
   from deltas.
5. Worker idle timeout vs queueing: a 300 s idle cap killed kimi-k3 workers still waiting in NIM's queue;
   a separate first-token budget (worker_first_token_timeout_seconds, 900 s) now applies until the first
   data event. Intent extraction on the fast routing got 120 s / 60 s idle (20 s killed every parse).
6. glm-5.3-flash queues 120 s before its first token on NIM, so it is no longer the fast routing
   (nemotron-3-ultra is; glm-5.3-flash stays as the glm alternate only).

7. The upstream enforces a 60-minute request ceiling: every glm-5.3 / deepseek-v4-flash high-effort failure
   on ARC and Apex landed at 3606-3641 s as "empty stream" (still reasoning when cut). Template: per-routing
   output caps (glm 24k tokens at medium effort, deepseek 40k, kimi 48k) so each member's stream can finish
   inside the ceiling, and the max-band worker cap is 3300 s so the kernel's own salvage fires first.

8. The proxy routes kimi-k3 differently by reasoning_effort: at "medium" the first token arrives after
   ~290-316 s (repeatedly measured against ~57-72 s for none/low/high), which is past the proxy's own
   admission timeout often enough that program slots at medium died with "All routes failed" — the v83-v86
   ARC runs had zero completed program slots for 70+ minutes. Template: reasoning_effort_by_routing kimi-k3
   → high; program slots back to "mixed" (deepseek's queue is effort-independent, ~240 s under our load).
9. The upstream admits ~4 concurrent requests per model and queues the rest, and its 60-minute ceiling
   counts queue time; a wave's burst (6 slots at once, or a release cascade after a settle) tripped its
   cooldown for every slot. Kernel: process-wide per-routing caps (kimi 2, deepseek 2 per server) with
   paced acquisitions (4 s apart), fast-failure re-dispatch 4 × 45 s.
10. A pipelined execution check verifies a proposal AFTER it landed, and the wave only consulted the early-
   settle rules once every family had a result: with kimi's slots queued, a verified deepseek program sat
   unused for 45 minutes. Kernel: verified programs consult the rules regardless, and the evidence wake
   is always registered.

Budgets sized to NIM (template): worker caps 600/1800/3300 s by band, search deadlines 900/2700/5400 s,
max-band extension 1800 s, contested extension 1200 s, synthesis 900 s, agentic planning 480 s.

Measurement protocol: members and candidates through the 9877 proxy (passthrough), reasoning_effort high,
64k output budget, 90-minute per-call cap, strict scoring (a failed or timed-out call is wrong); fusion
through the 9878 proxy on the template. Per-item tables: arcagi2-members-nim.txt, apex25-members-nim.txt,
bbeh-members-nim.txt, swebench-members-nim.txt (written as suites complete).
